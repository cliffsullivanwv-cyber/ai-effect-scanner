AI Effect in Education

Students weigh each AI scenario and hold up a card: Take the Trail, Approach with Caution, or Risky Terrain. One phone scans the whole room in seconds.

Step 1

Print cards

One numbered card per student. Same number every time. Step 2 · Classroom screen

Open the display

Shows the scenario, who has answered, and the reveal. Step 3 · Teacher phone

Scan the room

Point the camera at the class. Answers appear on the display live.

How a round works

  1. Show the scenario on the display. Students discuss it.
  2. Each student turns their card so their judgment is on top, then holds it up with the code facing you.
  3. Sweep the phone across the room. The display fills in as each card is read. The blank edge means "not answering".
  4. Tap Reveal to show how the class split, then have students make the case for their path.
